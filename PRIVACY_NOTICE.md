## Privacy and No Re-Identification Notice

Certain datasets included in this benchmark are derived from public-sector and open-data sources and may contain information that has been masked, obfuscated, pseudonymized, aggregated, or otherwise modified by Oracle.

Users must not:

- Attempt to identify or re-identify any individual represented in the benchmark data;
- Link, match, combine, or compare benchmark data with other datasets for the purpose of identifying individuals;
- Create individual profiles or dossiers;
- Use any benchmark data for advertising, marketing, customer targeting, eligibility determinations, credit, employment, housing, insurance, or other decisions concerning individuals; or
- Otherwise process any residual personal, pseudonymized, or individual-level information for purposes unrelated to evaluating, benchmarking, developing, or improving natural-language-to-Oracle-SQL or Oracle Analytic View SQL generation systems.

Users are responsible for complying with applicable laws, regulations, and the terms governing the original source datasets.

Any attempt to re-identify individuals or to use benchmark data for unrelated personal-data processing is outside the intended use of this benchmark.
